# Outcome Tracking Methodology

To transition CareerPilot from a mere "practice tool" to a statistically proven "placement prediction engine", real-world outcomes must be tracked.

## 1. The Challenge of Tracking
The platform inherently knows the student's mock interview scores, but it does not automatically know if the student actually gets hired by a company, as that happens outside the platform.

## 2. Tracking Mechanisms
- **Self-Reporting (Opt-In):** Post-placement season, students will receive an automated email asking them to optionally report if they received a job offer, the company name, and the role.
- **Placement Cell Integration (B2B):** The most reliable method. Universities utilizing the Coordinator Dashboard will be asked to upload an anonymized CSV of placement results at the end of the semester.

## 3. Data Anonymization
For research purposes, the mapping between the student's Career Readiness Score and their job offer will be anonymized using hashed user IDs. No PII (names/emails) will be included in the final academic research datasets.
