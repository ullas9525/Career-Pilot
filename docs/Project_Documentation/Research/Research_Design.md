# Research Design

## 1. Research Question
Does providing students with a zero-marginal-cost, AI-driven, highly contextualized mock interview platform significantly improve their objective "Career Readiness Score" over a 30-day period compared to baseline performance?

## 2. Study Type
- **Quantitative, Quasi-Experimental Pre-Test/Post-Test Design.**

## 3. Sampling
- **Target Population:** Final-Year B.Tech Computer Science students at the pilot university.
- **Sample Size ($N$):** Minimum of 100 students to achieve statistical significance.

## 4. Variables
- **Independent Variable (IV):** Exposure to the CareerPilot platform (specifically, the number of mock interviews completed within the 30-day window).
- **Dependent Variable (DV):** The final Career Readiness Score (derived from Interview Score and Resume Score).
- **Control Variables:** Target Role selected (Backend vs Frontend), baseline GPA (to ensure the improvement isn't purely correlated with academic intelligence).

## 5. Procedure
1. **Day 1 (Pre-Test):** All 100 students conduct one baseline mock interview using a generic "Software Engineer" Job Description. Scores are recorded but hidden from the students to prevent discouragement.
2. **Day 2-29 (Intervention):** Students are granted full access to the platform and encouraged to practice with real JDs from companies they wish to apply to.
3. **Day 30 (Post-Test):** Students conduct a final mock interview.
4. **Data Analysis:** Compare the mean Day 30 score to the mean Day 1 score using a paired sample t-test.
