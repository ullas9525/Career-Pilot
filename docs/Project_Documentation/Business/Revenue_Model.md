# Revenue Model

## Primary Revenue Stream: B2B Annual Licensing
CareerPilot monetizes exclusively through a B2B SaaS model targeting higher education institutions.
- **Pricing Structure:** Tiered annual licensing based on student cohort size.
  - *Tier 1 (Up to 250 students):* $X / year
  - *Tier 2 (Up to 1,000 students):* $Y / year
  - *Enterprise (Unlimited):* Custom pricing
- **Value Proposition:** The license fee is positioned against the college's existing spend on external mock interview agencies, representing a >80% cost reduction while providing 100x the scalability.

## Future Potential Revenue Streams (Phase 3+)
- **B2B2B Corporate Licensing:** Licensing the platform to HR departments for initial candidate screening before human interviews.
- **B2C Premium Upgrades:** While core access is provided via the college, students could potentially purchase premium "interview quota" top-ups if they exceed standard usage limits (not planned for MVP).
