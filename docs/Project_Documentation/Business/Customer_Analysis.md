# Customer Analysis

## 1. Target Customer Segments
CareerPilot operates on a B2B2C (Business-to-Business-to-Consumer) model.
- **B2B Customers:** Universities, Engineering Colleges, and Management Institutes. Specifically, the purchasing decision lies with the **Director of Placements** or the **Dean of Students**.
- **B2C End Users:** Final-year students actively seeking employment.

## 2. B2B Customer Needs (The Buyers)
- **High ROI:** They need tools that verifiably increase placement rates.
- **Scalability:** They need to assess 500+ students simultaneously without hiring 50 human interviewers.
- **Visibility:** They need dashboards to identify which students are struggling so they can intervene before company placement drives begin.
- **Affordability:** They operate on fixed annual budgets.

## 3. B2C End User Needs (The Consumers)
- **Anxiety Reduction:** A safe, judgment-free zone to practice speaking technical answers out loud.
- **Actionable Feedback:** Knowing exactly why an answer was wrong, and seeing what a "10/10" answer looks like.
- **Relevance:** Practicing with questions tailored to the exact Job Description they are applying for, rather than generic LeetCode questions.

## 4. Customer Objections & Mitigations
- **Objection:** "AI interviews aren't realistic."
  - **Mitigation:** Emphasize the audio-to-audio conversational loop driven by Nematron and DeepSeek, which features dynamic follow-ups rather than static quizzes.
- **Objection:** "Students might cheat."
  - **Mitigation:** Highlight the Phase 3 Anti-Cheating roadmap (dynamic probing of memorized answers) and the fact that mock interviews are for practice, not certification.
