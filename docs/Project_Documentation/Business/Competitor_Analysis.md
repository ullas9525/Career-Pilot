# Competitor Analysis

## 1. Direct Competitors
**Platforms (e.g., Pramp, Interviewing.io, Google Interview Warmup)**
- **Strengths:** Pramp offers free peer-to-peer interviews; Interviewing.io offers premium human-led FAANG interviews; Google Warmup offers basic ML-based text feedback.
- **Weaknesses:** Peer interviews lack expert feedback. Human-led interviews are unscalable and expensive ($150+/hr). Google Warmup is non-conversational (static text/audio inputs, no dynamic follow-ups).
- **CareerPilot Differentiator:** Audio-to-audio *dynamic* conversation with brutal, role-specific rubric scoring at near-zero cost.

## 2. Indirect Competitors
**Traditional Placement Prep Agencies (e.g., TIME, Career Launcher)**
- **Strengths:** Established university relationships, human touch.
- **Weaknesses:** High cost per student, generic curriculum, highly variable interviewer quality.
- **CareerPilot Differentiator:** Infinitely scalable, 100% objective, available 24/7 without scheduling friction.

## 3. Substitute Solutions
**Students practicing with ChatGPT directly**
- **Strengths:** Free, highly accessible.
- **Weaknesses:** Requires prompt engineering expertise; lacks a seamless audio loop; does not compute a standardized "Career Readiness Score" for university dashboards.
- **CareerPilot Differentiator:** Purpose-built UI, integrated resume context, strict rubric enforcement, and B2B tracking dashboards.
