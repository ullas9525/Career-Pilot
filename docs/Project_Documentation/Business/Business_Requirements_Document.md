# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Executive Summary
CareerPilot is a scalable, AI-driven mock interview platform aimed at bridging the gap between academic education and industry expectations. The business objective is to provide a zero-marginal-cost interview preparation tool to engineering and management colleges, thereby increasing their cohort placement rates and generating B2B SaaS licensing revenue.

## 2. Business Objectives
- **Objective 1:** Improve the average technical interview success rate of final-year students at partnered colleges by 15% within the first academic year.
- **Objective 2:** Reduce the per-student cost of mock interview preparation from $20 (human-led) to near-zero (AI-led).
- **Objective 3:** Convert 20% of free pilot colleges to paid B2B tier contracts within 6 months of the pilot conclusion.

## 3. Background and Needs
Colleges currently rely on alumni networks or expensive third-party agencies to conduct mock interviews. This approach does not scale, leaving the majority of students without realistic practice. As hiring freezes and industry standards tighten, colleges face immense pressure to prove their ROI through high placement percentages. CareerPilot addresses this by offering unlimited, hyper-realistic, objective mock interviews.

## 4. Scope
- **In Scope:** B2B2C model where colleges license the platform for their students. The platform includes voice-based AI interviews, role-specific rubrics, resume scoring, and coordinator dashboards.
- **Out of Scope (Current):** Direct B2C monetization (charging individual students), manual resume reviews by humans, placement guarantees, and automated job matching/recruitment services.

## 5. Stakeholders
- **Primary Sponsor:** University Deans / Directors of Placement.
- **End Users (Consumers):** Final-Year Students.
- **Administrators:** College Placement Coordinators.
- **Product Team:** CareerPilot Engineering and Product Teams.

## 6. Financial and Operational Constraints
- The platform infrastructure must operate on free tiers (Render, Neon, Groq, DeepSeek) during the MVP and pilot phases to ensure a zero-burn runway.
- B2B pricing must be structured on a per-student or per-cohort annual license to provide predictable revenue.
