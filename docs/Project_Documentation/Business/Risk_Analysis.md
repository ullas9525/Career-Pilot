# Risk Analysis

## 1. Technical Risks
- **Latency Spikes:** The STT -> LLM -> TTS pipeline may experience high latency during peak traffic, ruining the conversational flow.
  - *Mitigation:* The architecture is designed with Groq (ultra-fast STT) and Nematron (low-latency generation).
- **API Rate Limiting:** Relying heavily on free tiers could lead to abrupt service interruptions if quotas are exceeded.
  - *Mitigation:* Implement a "deployment-ready for pilot usage" approach that limits pilot cohort sizes. Post-pilot, shift to paid tiers backed by early revenue.

## 2. Product Risks
- **Score Inflation / Cheating:** Students may read off screens or memorize answers, leading to artificially high Career Readiness Scores.
  - *Mitigation:* Phase 3 will introduce adaptive anti-cheating mechanisms, including dynamic follow-up questioning that probes for deep understanding rather than memorization.
- **AI Hallucinations:** The AI interviewer may ask irrelevant questions or provide incorrect feedback.
  - *Mitigation:* Strict system prompts generated by DeepSeek using the specific Job Description and Resume data constrain Nematron's conversational space.

## 3. Business Risks
- **Long University Sales Cycles:** B2B sales to higher education institutions traditionally take 6-12 months.
  - *Mitigation:* Offer free 3-month pilot programs to bypass initial procurement hurdles, proving value (increased scores) before asking for budget.

## 4. Ethical & Compliance Risks
- **Data Privacy (GDPR/CCPA):** Handling student resumes, audio, and performance data carries severe privacy implications.
  - *Mitigation:* Audio and video are processed in-memory and deleted immediately post-interview. No media is stored. Students retain the right to delete their text transcripts and accounts at any time.
