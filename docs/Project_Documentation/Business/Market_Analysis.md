# Market Analysis

## 1. Industry Overview
The EdTech market, specifically the higher-education and employability segment, is undergoing a massive shift. With the rise of AI, traditional rote-learning assessments are becoming obsolete. Employers are demanding practical, communication-heavy, and problem-solving skills, verified through rigorous technical interviews.

## 2. Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- **Global:** Thousands of engineering and management institutions worldwide graduating millions of students annually who require technical interview preparation.
- **Serviceable Available Market (SAM):** Tier-2 and Tier-3 engineering colleges that lack the elite alumni networks of Tier-1 institutions, resulting in lower baseline placement rates and a higher desperation for automated, high-quality training tools.

## 3. Market Trends
- **AI-Driven Assessment:** Companies (e.g., HireVue) already use AI to screen candidates. Students need to practice interacting with AI interfaces.
- **Cost Reduction in EdTech:** Universities are cutting budgets for external training vendors, demanding software-based scalability.
- **Skill-Based Hiring:** Shift from GPA-based screening to portfolio and interview-based screening, emphasizing the need for realistic mock interview practice.

## 4. Market Drivers
- **Intense Job Competition:** Tech layoffs and tightening hiring pipelines make every interview a high-stakes event for students.
- **B2B Purchasing Power:** Universities are heavily incentivized to spend money on tools that increase their placement percentages, as this directly affects their national rankings and future admissions.
