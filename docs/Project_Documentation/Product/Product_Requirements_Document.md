# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Document Overview
**Product Name:** CareerPilot
**Document Status:** Approved (MVP Phase)
**Target Audience:** Final-Year Engineering/Management Students, College Placement Cells

## 2. Objective
To build an AI-driven, voice-conversational mock interview platform that prepares students for technical interviews by simulating real-world conditions based on specific Job Descriptions (JDs) and student resumes. The platform will evaluate students against objective rubrics and provide actionable feedback, all while operating at a near-zero marginal cost using free-tier infrastructure.

## 3. High-Level Requirements

### 3.1 Student Workflows
- **Authentication:** Standard Email/Password registration and login.
- **Role Selection:** Ability to select target roles (e.g., Backend, Frontend, PM, Data Science).
- **Interview Setup:** Ability to input a target Company Name and paste a specific Job Description (JD).
- **Resume Integration:** The system automatically fetches the student's previously uploaded resume details (projects, tech stack, and resume score).
- **Live Interview:**
  - Full audio-to-audio conversational interface.
  - Speech-to-Text (STT) for student inputs.
  - Text-to-Speech (TTS) for the AI interviewer's voice.
  - Context-aware questioning (driving off the JD, company data, and resume).
- **Results & Feedback:**
  - Post-interview computation of the **Career Readiness Score** (60% Interview, 30% Resume, 10% Consistency).
  - Detailed score breakdown by rubric dimensions.
  - Side-by-side transcript review (Student answer vs. Ideal answer).
  - Generation of a 14-day improvement plan.

### 3.2 Placement Coordinator Workflows
- **Dashboard:** High-level metrics view (Total interviews conducted, average readiness score).
- **Student Roster:** List of all students with their individual readiness scores.
- **Alerts:** Automated flagging of "at-risk" students who consistently score below a predefined threshold.
- **Outcome Tracking (Optional):** Ability to log actual job offers to validate predictive hypotheses.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **User Engagement:** Number of mock interviews completed per student per week.
- **System Latency:** End-to-end latency of the live interview audio loop (Target: < 2 seconds).
- **Accuracy:** Relevance of questions generated by the AI relative to the provided JD.
- **Adoption:** Percentage of the cohort actively using the platform.

## 5. Constraints & Assumptions
- **Infrastructure:** The entire MVP must run on free tiers (Render, Neon, Groq, DeepSeek, Nematron).
- **Cost:** Marginal cost must remain near-zero during the MVP stage assuming usage remains within free-tier limits.
- **Privacy:** Audio and video streams are strictly ephemeral; they are processed in-memory and deleted immediately upon session end. Only textual transcripts and scores are retained.

## 6. Out of Scope (For MVP)
- Adaptive Anti-Cheating mechanisms (Phase 3).
- Facial expression / video confidence analysis.
- Multi-language support.
- Native mobile applications (Web-only for MVP).
