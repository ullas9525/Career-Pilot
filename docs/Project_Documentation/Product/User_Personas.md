# User Personas

## Persona 1: The Anxious Student
**Name:** Rahul Sharma
**Role:** Final-Year B.Tech Student (Computer Science)
**Goal:** Secure a high-paying Software Engineering role at a top-tier product company.
**Pain Points:**
- High technical knowledge but freezes during verbal technical interviews.
- Cannot articulate trade-offs clearly when asked open-ended architecture questions.
- Does not have access to experienced industry mentors for regular mock interviews.
- Finds peer mock interviews unhelpful as peers lack deep interviewing experience.

**How CareerPilot Helps:**
Provides an intimidating-free environment to practice speaking technical concepts aloud. Gives brutal, objective feedback on *how* to structure answers (e.g., using STAR method) without the social embarrassment of failing in front of a human.

## Persona 2: The Overworked Placement Coordinator
**Name:** Dr. Ananya Patel
**Role:** Head of Training and Placements
**Goal:** Maximize the college's placement percentage and average CTC.
**Pain Points:**
- Tasked with preparing 500+ students for interviews.
- No budget to hire external agencies for personalized mock interviews.
- Lacks visibility into which students are actually prepared versus those who are just attending classes.
- Cannot manually track student progress across different technical domains (Frontend, Backend, PM).

**How CareerPilot Helps:**
Provides a scalable platform that automatically assesses the entire cohort at zero marginal cost. The dashboard highlights "at-risk" students, allowing Dr. Patel to focus her limited human intervention time on the students who need it most, based on objective "Career Readiness Scores."
