# Success Metrics

To objectively measure the success of the CareerPilot platform during the MVP and pilot phases, we will track the following metrics categorized by user type.

## Student-Level Metrics
- **Activation Rate:** Percentage of registered students who complete at least one full mock interview.
- **Engagement (Practice Consistency):** Average number of mock interviews completed per student per month.
- **Skill Progression:** Month-over-month percentage increase in the average Career Readiness Score (tracking individual improvement).
- **Completion Rate:** Percentage of started mock interviews that reach the final feedback screen (measuring drop-offs due to technical errors or user frustration).

## Coordinator/College-Level Metrics (B2B)
- **Cohort Readiness Improvement:** Aggregate shift in the median Career Readiness Score for the entire batch.
- **At-Risk Identification Accuracy:** Number of students correctly flagged as "at-risk" who subsequently received targeted mentoring.
- **Placement Correlation (Hypothesis Validation):** *Note: This is tracked long-term based on optional outcome reporting.* The statistical correlation between high Career Readiness Scores and actual placement job offers.

## System Performance & Financial Metrics
- **End-to-End Latency:** Average time from the student stopping their audio recording to the AI interviewer beginning its audio response.
  - *Target:* < 3.0 seconds.
- **System Uptime:** Percentage of time the core interview loop is available (measured via UptimeRobot).
- **Cost per Interview:** Marginal cost incurred per completed interview session.
  - *Target:* Near-zero cost during MVP stage assuming usage remains within free-tier limits.
- **API Error Rate:** Percentage of STT, TTS, or LLM generation calls that fail or timeout.

## Product/Business Success
- **Pilot-to-Paid Conversion:** Percentage of colleges that convert from a free pilot program to a paid licensing contract.
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score):** Student satisfaction score collected post-interview.
