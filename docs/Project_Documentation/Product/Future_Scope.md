# Future Scope (Post-MVP)

## Phase 2: Enhanced Capabilities & Gamification
Once the core platform is proven and stable, the following features will be introduced to increase engagement and expand the platform's utility:
- **LinkedIn / GitHub Profile Audits:** Automated AI evaluation of the student's digital footprint to ensure consistency with their uploaded resume.
- **Peer-to-Peer Mock Interviews:** A matchmaking system that pairs students to conduct mock interviews for each other, using CareerPilot's rubrics as a guide.
- **Company-Specific Question Licensing:** Partnerships with hiring platforms or companies to provide proprietary, verified question banks.
- **Faculty Dashboards:** Enhanced curriculum feedback loops, allowing professors to see aggregate skill gaps (e.g., "The entire CSE batch is scoring poorly on System Design, modify the syllabus").
- **Multi-Language Support:** Translating the UI and supporting localized STT/TTS for regional engineering colleges.

## Phase 3: Adaptive Intelligence & Anti-Cheating
To ensure the integrity of the "Career Readiness Score" and prevent score inflation, the following advanced AI mechanisms will be introduced:
- **Dynamic Follow-Up Questioning:** The AI will aggressively probe shallow answers. If a student recites a memorized definition (e.g., "FastAPI is faster"), the AI will demand deep technical justification ("Explain the event loop and how asynchronous execution improves performance").
- **Resume Consistency Checks:** The AI will cross-reference live answers with resume claims to detect contradictions.
- **Context-Aware Challenge Questions:** Introducing deliberate curveballs or edge-case constraints mid-interview to test adaptive problem solving.

## Features Explicitly Avoided
- **Video Facial-Expression / Confidence Analysis:** Excluded due to inherent AI bias, lack of scientific validity in hiring, and massive compute costs. The focus remains strictly on technical competence and communication structure.
- **Full Anxiety-Management Module:** Breathing exercises and real-time biometric anxiety tracking are deferred indefinitely, as the primary goal is technical preparation, not medical/psychological intervention.
